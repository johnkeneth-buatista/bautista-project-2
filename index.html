A Legacy of Faith and Education

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has served as the home of Catholic education in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental.

  • Nearly a century of Catholic education and community formation.
  • Academic excellence grounded in faith, character, and service.
  • Graduates prepared to lead, serve, and make a positive difference.
