Bachelor of Science in Criminology

The KCC Bachelor of Science in Criminology program prepares students for professional service in public safety, law enforcement, corrections, investigation, and related fields.

Learning combines academic foundations, discipline, leadership, ethical values, practical formation, and community responsibility within the Catholic identity of KCC.

What You'll Learn

  • Criminal justice systems and institutions
  • Crime detection and investigation fundamentals
  • Forensic science and evidence handling
  • Law enforcement administration
  • Leadership and disciplined public service
  • Criminal law and legal procedures
  • Research and criminological studies
  • Ethics and community responsibility

Professional preparation with purpose

The program develops knowledge, discipline, leadership, and service.

Students study criminal justice, law, investigation, forensic science, and public safety while strengthening judgment and responsible citizenship.

Learning inside and beyond the classroom

Academic instruction is reinforced by practical formation.

Students build competencies through coursework, simulations, research, physical conditioning, leadership activities, and professional expectations.

Opportunities in public safety and justice

Graduates may pursue roles aligned with their training and qualifications.

Possible career directions include law enforcement, corrections, investigation, security, forensic support, and public service.

Formed to lead with integrity

Professional competence begins with Christian character.

KCC formation emphasizes discipline, compassion, respect for human dignity, ethical judgment, and service to the community.
